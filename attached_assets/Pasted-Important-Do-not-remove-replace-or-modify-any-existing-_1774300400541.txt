Important: Do not remove, replace, or modify any existing features or functionality that is currently working. Only add the Super Admin dashboard described below. Keep all existing features exactly as they are.
Build a complete Super Admin dashboard accessible only to accounts with a superadmin role. This dashboard is completely separate from the regular GC and Sub experience. Here are the exact requirements:

Add a superadmin boolean field to the User table. Superadmin users access the admin dashboard at the route /admin. No regular GC or Sub user can access this route — redirect them to their normal dashboard if they try.
The first screen at /admin shows a morning summary with these live numbers: MRR, total paying customers, active trials, trials expiring within 7 days, customers churned this month, MRR lost to churn, accounts at or near their subcontract limit, accounts with churn risk indicators, and failed payments. Every number is a clickable link to the relevant detail list.
Build a full customer list table showing every account with company name, owner name, owner email, current plan, billing frequency, account status, trial expiry date if applicable, active subcontract count vs limit, total projects, last login date, signup date, and MRR contribution. The table is sortable by every column and filterable by status, plan, and risk level. Each row has an Impersonate button.
The Impersonate feature allows the superadmin to tap Impersonate on any account and immediately see the app exactly as that GC or Sub sees it. Show a persistent red banner at the top of the screen that says: Admin View — Impersonating company name — with an Exit Impersonation button. The superadmin can navigate the entire app as that user. No actions taken during impersonation send real emails or charge real payments.
Build a Trial Pipeline section showing all trial accounts grouped by days remaining — 1 to 3 days, 4 to 7 days, 8 to 11 days, 12 to 14 days, and expired trials. For each trial show company name, owner email, what actions they have taken in the app, last activity date, days remaining, a Send Follow Up Email button, and a notes field for the superadmin to add private notes about that account.
Build a Churn Risk section showing all paying customers scored as red high risk, yellow medium risk, or green healthy. Red means no login in 14 days or payment failed. Yellow means login frequency dropping or no new subcontracts in 60 days. Show the specific reason for each risk flag and a Send Check In Email button for each account.
Build an Upgrade Opportunities section showing all accounts at 70 percent or more of their subcontract limit. Show their current plan, current count, limit, and an Offer Upgrade button that sends them a direct link to the billing page with the recommended next plan pre-selected.
Build a Product Usage section showing across all accounts: how many GCs have uploaded plans, run AI analysis, created trade scopes, enabled retention, used compliance checklists, and how many subs have confirmed payments and signed lien waivers.
Build a Support Queue section showing all accounts with failed payments in the last 7 days, accounts where the GC has never logged in after signup, and disputed payments unresolved for more than 48 hours.
Build a Financial Overview section showing total revenue collected this month and year, failed payments and amount at risk, upcoming renewals in the next 30 days with customer name and amount, and revenue broken down by plan.
Build an Email Tools section where the superadmin can send a direct email to any single customer, or a broadcast email to a segment — all trials, all paying customers, all churned customers, all annual customers. Show full email history per account including open status.
Build a Demo Management section where the superadmin can reset the GC demo data and Sub demo data with one tap, and see how many people clicked the demo buttons in the last 7 and 30 days and how many converted to real signups.
Build a Feature Flags section where the superadmin can toggle any major feature on or off globally or on a per-account basis without touching the codebase.
The superadmin dashboard is completely mobile responsive — the morning summary and trial pipeline must be fully usable on a phone.
