Important: Do not remove, replace, or modify any existing features or functionality that is currently working. Only remove the Direct Award flow and replace it with the standard bid invitation flow as described below.
Multiple GCs have given feedback that the Direct Award — No Bid Required flow is wrong. GCs do not want to automatically give a sub the job. They want to invite the sub to bid even when adding them mid-construction. Remove the Direct Award flow completely and replace it with the standard Invite Sub to Bid flow at every phase.
STEP 1 — Remove the Direct Award modal and everything connected to it. Delete the Direct Award Amount field, the Direct Award Subcontract button, and any API route that creates a subcontract directly without a bid. Remove all references to Direct Award from the entire codebase.
STEP 2 — When a GC adds a sub during the CONSTRUCTION phase the flow must be identical to adding a sub during the BIDDING phase. The GC selects a sub from their contact book or enters a new sub email. The sub receives a magic link invitation email. The sub logs in and submits their own bid amount. The GC reviews the bid and accepts it. The subcontract is created from the sub's own submitted number. No shortcuts. No GC entering the contract amount.
STEP 3 — The only difference between adding a sub during BIDDING and adding a sub during CONSTRUCTION is the label on the invitation. During CONSTRUCTION phase the invitation email to the sub should say: You have been invited to submit a bid for additional scope on project name which is currently under construction. Please review the scope details and submit your bid amount. The GC will review and respond promptly.
STEP 4 — On the project page during CONSTRUCTION phase the Add Subcontractor button or Invite Sub to Bid button must work exactly the same as it does during BIDDING phase. Same flow. Same screens. Same bid submission process. No separate construction phase invite flow.
STEP 5 — Remove any text anywhere in the app that says bypasses the normal bidding process or No Bid Required or Direct Award. These phrases should not exist anywhere in SubTracker.
STEP 6 — Confirm by testing the full flow during construction phase: tap Invite Sub to Bid, select a sub, confirm the sub receives a magic link invitation, log in as the sub and submit a bid amount, log back in as the GC and accept the bid, confirm a subcontract is created using the sub's own submitted amount with no GC-entered contract amount anywhere in the flow.
