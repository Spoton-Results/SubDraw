Then call stripe.accountLinks.create with account set to the new account ID, refresh_url set to the app URL plus /account/payment-setup, return_url set to the app URL plus /account/payment-success, type account_onboarding. Return the URL and immediately redirect the sub to the Stripe hosted onboarding page. Stripe handles all identity verification, bank account collection, and compliance. GCs can now pay you directly to your bank account. If not yet verified show — Stripe is still reviewing your information. We will notify you when your account is ready.
